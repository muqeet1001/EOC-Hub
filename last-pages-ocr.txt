EQUAL OPPORTUNITY CELL (EOC)
The Equal Opportunity Cell encompasses various dedicated cells to support and
empower our students, including:
1. SC/ST Cell:A dedicated cell for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes to address their specific
educational andsocial needs.
2.OBC Cell:A cell focused on the Other Backward Classes, ensuring their representation and
addressing any challenges they face in education.
3. Women's Cell: A cell aimed at promoting gender equality and addressing issues related to female
students, including safety, harassment, and empowerment.
4. Minority Cell: A cell focused on the needs of religious and linguistic minorities to ensure their
inclusion and support in the educational system.
5.DifferentlyAbledCell:A specializedgroup thataddresses theneedsofstudentswithdisabilities,
ensuring accessibility and accommodations in education.
6. International Students' Cell: A cell dedicated to supporting foreign students in navigating the
educational system, cultural integration, and addressing any challenges they may face.
7. Counselling and Mentoring Cell: A cell aimed at providing psychological support and mentoring to
students from various backgrounds to help them cope with academic and social pressures.
8. Gender Sensitization Cell: The GSSHP Cell aims to create awareness about gender issues among
students, faculty, and staff, promoting understanding of gender equality, rights, and responsibilities.
9.Prevention of Sexual Harassment Cell:The cell is tasked with implementingmeasures to prevent
sexual harassment within the institution, ensuring that complaints are taken seriously and handled
appropriately.
10. National Service Scheme (NSS): A program designed to engage students in community service and
social outreach initiatives to promote a sense of responsibility and social awareness.
11.Human Studies Cell: A research unit focused on conducting ethical studies involving human
participants to explore behavior, health, and social dynamics.
12.PopulationStudiesCell:Ananalytical body thatexamines demographictrends,healthoutcomes,
and societal impacts through statistical analysis of population data.
13. Remedial Coaching Cell: A supportive educational unit that provides additional tutoring and
assistance to students struggling academically to help them improve their performance.
14.Women's Study Centre:A dedicated centre that promotes research,education,and awareness about
women's issues, gender equity, and women's rights within the academic community.
